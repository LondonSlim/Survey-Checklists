Insurance Survey · 1 May 2024 · Hugh Ellacott, Isis Marine

0 of 19 items reviewed
Recommendations
Seal hatch into bow sponson with bolts and a seal.
Fit lifelines to stanchions around bow sponson.
Consider undertaking a stability assessment.
Ensure wire gates for emergency escape ladders on upper deck are fastened closed.
Provide two fire extinguishers on upper and lower deck accommodation.
Wall mount fire blankets in lower deck and upper deck kitchens.
Ensure there are an appropriate number of carbon monoxide alarms and smoke alarms in accommodation.
Rearrange gas locker door on lower deck so that it can be opened however vessel is moored.
Sheath lower deck gas locker with GRP.
Shorten gas hose in lower deck gas locker to no more than 1m in length.
Designate area for storage of spare gas bottles on upper deck that is 1m away from any openings into accommodation.
Sheath upper deck gas locker with GRP.
Shorten gas hose to 1m or less in upper deck gas locker.
Modify joinery next to gas shut-off valve in upper deck kitchen so that valve can be fully closed.
Increase fixed ventilation provision on lower deck.
Increase fixed ventilation provision in upper deck kitchen.
Advice
Ensure deck of bow sponson is fully covered with chequer plate.
Tilt outboard engines to raise legs out of water.
Refit bubble tester in upper deck gas locker.
