Advice
Osmotic blisters can be treated when the vessel is next ashore: open, repeatedly wash with water, dry laminate and fill cavity with marine epoxy filler.
Remove loose antifouling from underbody and paint with at least two coats of antifouling, next time the vessel is ashore.
Improve electrical connections between the starboard hull anode and outboard starboard stern gear.
Replace all existing anodes with aluminium anodes next time the vessel is ashore.
Fit a smoke detector; one with a hush button deals with nuisance alarms.
Remove perished noise insulation from engine compartment.
Consider replacing primary fuel filters with a metal bowl or plastic bowl that has an intrinsic fire resistance of 2½ minutes at 600°C (marked ISO 10088).
Make good join between discharge hose and aft hand basin waste using a stainless steel hose clamp.
Confirm whether the mains installation has an RCD. Install an RCD for all mains power sources if any do not have such protection.

Island Gypsy 40: common defects
Osmosis: compare with 2025. Moisture index below waterline 62–100 (shallow) and 45–100 (deep); topsides 17–21. Blisters up to 75mm on underbody and keel, white epoxy coating in places soft. Check whether blisters have been treated, and the state of the coating.
Cored topsides: elevated shallow moisture readings on lowest 'plank' to starboard, between engine room and bow. Retest.
Teak decks screwed and bonded to cored deck: missing plugs, caulking not adhering. Screws let water into the core. Tread test for soft spots; check deck around fillers and stanchion feet.
Mild steel fuel tanks (port and starboard): check tops for rust, especially beneath filler pipes, and for any attempt to disguise corrosion. Rust on tank tops often points to deck leaks.
Fresh water tank: type and condition not recorded in 2025. Steel tanks on the range are prone to rust, particularly on top. Identify and inspect.
Wheelhouse sliding door (starboard) and windows: sliding doors on the IG40 have been reported to leak. Look for staining and damaged panelling around door and window frames.
Flybridge-to-deck join and moulding above windscreen: reported to open and let water in. Check for staining, sealant failure and damp below.
Hull to deck join: only a small section seen in 2025 (chain locker). Check as much as possible.
Metal through-hulls and fittings bonded into laminate: check security and laminate around them, including strainers screwed to the hull.
Past osmosis repairs: assess quality and extent of the epoxy treatment and any previous repair work.
