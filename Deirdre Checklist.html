Recommendations
Repack the rudder post.
Resecure the rudder post to the plate rudder with oversize bolts.
Replace the damaged propeller.
Change the sealant on the solid fuel stove to a high-temperature flexible sealant.
Install CO and smoke detector alarms.
Tighten the drive belt to the upper alternator and water pump.
Secure the batteries in a vented box, ideally vented to the outside atmosphere.
Fit a new starter battery — the existing one was discharged and may be damaged.
Fit an RCD in the AC circuit, if not already fitted.
Plug the unused skin fitting in the engine room.
Address the navigation light configuration — it does not conform with the International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea.
Advice
Add a pair of magnesium anodes roughly amidships (consider aluminium/Navalloy anodes if the boat spends time in brackish or heavily polluted water).
Consider replacing the cutlass bearing — not essential; depending on use it may last up to 5 years.
Consider changing the isolator switch to the positive cable, and fitting an isolator switch on the starting battery.
Investigate increasing airflow to the cooker burners — flames showed yellow tips, suggesting reduced airflow or excessive gas supply.
Consider adding a means to shut off fuel to the engine without opening the engine space, such as a wire lanyard to the wheelhouse from a right-angle valve at the tank outlet.
